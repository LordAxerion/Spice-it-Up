# World War Ship

world war ship is a work in progress mod about war in anno 1800 game for now you are able to build 8 ships from ww1 to ww2 and more will come in the future.
And to build them you have a full new advanced shipyard with crazy details.

## Requirements

None.

## Gameplay

### World War Ship

The mod is release with 8 new ships. destroyer, corvette, submarine, cruiser, utility ship, pre-dreadnought, yamato, aircraft carrier

All those ships have their own skills and cost. They will lead your navy into war. you can find tutorials and presentation here: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC2v9Y_XFGdzTORY-xbawRcA.
